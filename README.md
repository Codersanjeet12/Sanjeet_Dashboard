# 📊 Power BI Dashboards — IT Hub Sales & Team Performance

Two interactive **Power BI** reports built to turn raw tabular data into quick, decision-ready insights: a **sales dashboard** (revenue, quantity, profit, payment mode) and a **team performance report** (runs, wickets, home city map).

> Built by **Sanjeet Kumar** — Data Science & AI learner | Ex-Intern, Nexthikes IT Solutions

---

## 🔍 Dashboard 1 — Sanjeet IT Hub Sales Dashboard

![IT Hub Sales Dashboard](screenshots/it-hub-sales-dashboard.png)

**KPI cards:** Total Quantity (**19**), Total Amount (**~1K**), Average Daily Value / ADV (**397.19**)

**Visuals & insights**
| Visual | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Sum of Amount by State | Revenue split by state (Haryana) |
| Sum of Amount by CustomerName | Top customers by spend (Aniket, Apoorva) |
| Sum of Quantity by Category | Furniture (10) > Clothing (7) > Electronics (2) |
| Sum of Quantity by PaymentMode | Debit Card (17) vs COD (2) — digital payments dominate |
| Sum of Profit by Month | Month-wise profit trend (Oct vs Nov — Nov clearly higher) |
| Sum of Profit by Sub-Category | Profit contribution of each sub-category with range slider |

**Interactivity:** Quarter slicer (Qtr 1–4), State dropdown filter, cross-filtering across all visuals, custom dark theme with highlighted KPI cards.

---

## 🔍 Dashboard 2 — Team Performance Report

![Team Performance Report](screenshots/team-performance-report.png)

- **Table:** Sum of Wickets & Sum of Runs for 11 teams (total **869 wickets / 23,945 runs**)
- **Pie chart:** Share of total runs by team
- **Map (Home_City):** Geographic view of each team's home city
- **Q&A visual:** Ask questions in plain English (e.g. "most recent order", "maximum profit")
- **Slicer:** Click a team to filter table, pie and map together
